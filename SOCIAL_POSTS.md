## LinkedIn

AI scams no longer arrive with obvious spelling mistakes. A cloned voice can
sound like someone you trust, and a polished message can still lead somewhere
dangerous.

I built **FlipSec.ai** for the Convex All Gas Hackathon: an AI security learning
feed for people who are not security experts.

Every card starts with a real report in plain language. Flip it to see how the
threat worked, what gave it away, why it convinced someone careful, and a short
exercise you can try yourself.

FlipSec also includes:

- AI Sec News, Learn, and Jobs feeds
- Ask FlipSec for calm, practical safety guidance
- Card translations across 11 languages
- Natural read-aloud audio and accessibility controls
- Daily email lessons you can answer in your own words. The AI grades the
  answer in the same thread, and you can keep replying with safety questions
  for contextual Ask FlipSec answers without reopening the app.

The sponsor stack performs the actual product work:

- **@firecrawl** collects public source material.
- **@OpenAI** creates plain-language explanations, translations, lessons,
  natural voice, and reply feedback.
- **@convex** runs the realtime database, queries, mutations, scheduled
  pipelines, Agent threads, file storage, HTTP webhooks, and public hosting.
- **@agentmail** makes email a two-way AI interface. It sends each lesson,
  receives answers and follow-up questions, and delivers the AI response in
  the same email thread.
